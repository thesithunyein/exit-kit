# Code of Conduct

## Standards

Treat other contributors with respect. Discuss the work, give specific feedback, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, threats, and sharing someone else's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to Exit Kit issues, pull requests, and other spaces used to collaborate on the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the [maintainer](mailto:sithunyein.mailto@gmail.com?subject=Exit%20Kit%20conduct%20report). Include links or a description of the behavior if you can share them safely. The maintainer will review the report, limit disclosure to what is needed to address it, and may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation depending on the circumstances.

This policy draws on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
