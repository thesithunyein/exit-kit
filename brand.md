# Exit Kit

- **Mark** supplied white angular K on black
- **Canvas** #101010, with #171717 surfaces and #0A0A0A for recessed areas
- **Text** #F4F4F1 and secondary #ABABA7
- **Rule** #343434
- **Action** off-white with near-black text. Color is reserved for transaction and network states.
- **Type** Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono for identifiers
- **Layout** an editorial opening with a large mark and parallel diagonals. A compact action pane sits beside a wider receipt pane, stacked on small screens.
- **Voice** describe the status and give the user their next action in plain language

Feasy informed the stable loading geometry and compact wallet states. Altitude and Jeremy Blaze’s payment timeline informed the editorial grid and receipt hierarchy. Exit Kit uses its own visual identity.
