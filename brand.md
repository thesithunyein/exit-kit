# Exit Kit

- **Canvas** paper #F3F1EB and surface #FCFBF8
- **Text** ink #1D2524 and muted #596361
- **Rule** #D8DCD5
- **Action** blue #214FBE
- **Type** Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono for identifiers
- **Layout** an action pane beside a wider receipt pane, stacked on small screens
- **Voice** describe the status and give the user their next action in plain language

Feasy informed the stable loading geometry and compact wallet states. Altitude and Jeremy Blaze’s payment timeline informed the editorial grid and receipt hierarchy. Exit Kit uses its own visual identity.
