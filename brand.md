# Exit Kit

- **Mark** supplied white angular K on black
- **Canvas** #0B0F12, with #13191D surfaces and #101619 for recessed areas
- **Text** #EFF3F2 and secondary #A3AFB2
- **Rule** #2B373C
- **Action** muted cyan #A1DDDA with #102524 text. Green, amber, and red distinguish verified payouts, pending finality, and errors.
- **Type** Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono for identifiers
- **Layout** a restrained introduction with the original mark framed by soft geometric outlines. File selection and withdrawal details sit side by side on desktop and stack in reading order on mobile.
- **Voice** describe the status and give the user their next action in plain language

Feasy informed the stable loading geometry and compact wallet states. Altitude and Jeremy Blaze’s payment timeline informed the editorial grid and receipt hierarchy. Exit Kit uses its own visual identity.
